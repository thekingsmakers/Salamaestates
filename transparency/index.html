Data Quality & Public Transparency Policy

We believe users deserve complete transparency regarding the source of information. Learn how listings are validated, our quality benchmarks, and guidance for verifying property specifics.

Transparency First

Real estate decisions represent significant financial commitments for families, businesses, and investors. Salama Estates is committed to open, unambiguous communication regarding where listing information originates and how it is verified.

“We believe our community deserves complete transparency regarding the source, scope, and validation of all property data displayed on Salama Estates.”

Some listings currently displayed on our marketplace are collected from publicly available real estate sources as part of our initial launch initiative. Below, we detail our validation safeguards and the limitations users should understand.

Data Quality & Validation Process

Data accuracy is paramount in real estate. Information gathered from public channels undergoes verification and normalization before publication whenever possible.

Validated fields typically include:

Property Title
Property Price & Currency
Location & Coordinates
Property Classification
Bedroom Count
Bathroom Count
Key Amenities & Features
Public Property Images
Originating Agency Information
Description & Specifications

Recognizing Public Data Limitations

While public data is generally accurate and reflective of the current market, some imported listings may have inherent limitations:

Public Listing Considerations

  • Limited Descriptions: Public listings may omit detailed architectural or lease conditions.
  • Fewer Images: High-resolution galleries or floor plans may not be publicly accessible.
  • Missing Specifics: Service charges, pet policies, or utility connections may require direct confirmation.
  • Delayed Status Changes: When a property is sold, let, or reserved on an external website, there may be a brief delay before that status is mirrored here.

User Advice: Before making financial commitments, signing contracts, or transferring deposits, always confirm critical property specifications, availability, and pricing directly with the property owner or registered agent.

Property Owner & Agency Rights

Salama Estates respects the rights of property owners, landlords, and licensed agencies. We provide expedited processes for:

Claiming a Listing: If your property or agency listing is displayed, you can claim ownership, update details, add floor plans, and route all leads directly to your inbox.
Requesting Removal: Property owners or exclusive listing agents may request immediate removal or status updates of any listing via our support desk.
Upgrading to Direct Listing: Replace standard imported listings with high-definition, verified direct listings featuring virtual tours and direct inquiry buttons.

Official Transparency Statement

Our Integrity Guarantee

Salama Estates does not falsify property data, manipulate pricing trends, or create artificial availability. Imported data serves solely to populate market visibility during our early growth phase.

Our long-term commitment is a marketplace powered 100% by direct listings published by verified property owners, licensed agencies, and certified developers.
