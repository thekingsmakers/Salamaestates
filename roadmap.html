Strategic Roadmap Active Execution 2026 - 2027

Platform Growth Roadmap & Future Vision

Our multi-stage plan to transition from an early-stage launch initiative into the region's largest, fully user-driven property ecosystem.

The Future Vision

Moving To A Fully Community-Driven Marketplace

The current approach of displaying verified imported property listings is a temporary launch strategy designed to provide immediate value to visitors.

Our ultimate vision is a platform powered entirely by property owners, licensed real estate agents, agencies, and developers. As our verified community grows, imported listings will be steadily phased out.

“We are constructing an open, self-sustaining real estate infrastructure where every listing is maintained directly by its legitimate owner or authorized agent.”

Milestone Timeline

Property Marketplace Public Launch

Initial platform deployment. Includes the temporary property data acquisition initiative, foundational search architecture, property taxonomy, and responsive search for buyers and renters across all desktop and mobile devices.
