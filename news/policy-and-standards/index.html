Salama Estates Cybersecurity & Security Monitoring Policy

Salama Estates maintains a comprehensive cybersecurity and security monitoring framework designed to protect residents, staff, properties, information systems, and community facilities. Our approach combines access controls, security monitoring, system logging, incident detection, and appropriate physical security measures to identify and respond to unauthorized access, suspicious activity, and potential security incidents.

“Protecting our communities means protecting both the people who live in them and the information and systems they rely on.”

1. Cybersecurity Program Overview

Salama Estates operates a cybersecurity program designed to protect company systems, property-management platforms, resident information, business records, connected devices, and other technology resources.

Security controls are implemented according to the sensitivity of the information and systems involved. These controls may include strong authentication, access restrictions, encryption, security updates, endpoint protection, network monitoring, backups, and administrative controls.

Access to Salama Estates systems is provided according to job responsibilities and business requirements. Personnel are expected to use company systems responsibly and must not attempt to bypass security controls or access information without authorization.

2. Security Monitoring & System Logs

Salama Estates maintains security logs and monitoring capabilities to help identify unauthorized access, suspicious activity, system failures, and potential cybersecurity incidents.

Depending on the system, security logs may record events such as authentication attempts, successful and unsuccessful logins, account changes, administrative activity, system alerts, access to protected resources, network security events, and other relevant security information.

Security logs are reviewed through automated alerts, security monitoring tools, and authorized personnel. Monitoring is performed for legitimate security, operational, compliance, and incident-response purposes.

Log access is restricted to authorized personnel with a legitimate business or security requirement. Logs may be retained for an appropriate period based on legal, regulatory, operational, and security requirements.

3. Unauthorized Access & Trespassing Detection

Salama Estates uses appropriate physical and technological security measures to help identify unauthorized access to controlled areas and property.

Depending on the location and applicable requirements, security measures may include access-control systems, entry records, security cameras, alarms, visitor-management systems, security personnel, and other monitoring technologies.

When an unauthorized person is detected in a restricted area, relevant security information may be reviewed to establish what occurred, when the event occurred, which access point or system was involved, and what response is appropriate.

Security personnel and authorized management may review relevant access records and surveillance information when investigating suspected trespassing, unauthorized entry, property damage, theft, or other security incidents.

Monitoring does not replace emergency services or law enforcement. Where an incident presents an immediate threat to people or property, appropriate emergency procedures will be followed.

4. Identity & Access Management

Access to Salama Estates systems, facilities, applications, and sensitive information is controlled according to the principle of least privilege.

Users receive only the access required to perform their authorized responsibilities. Access rights may be reviewed periodically and should be removed or modified when an individual's role changes or access is no longer required.

Administrative accounts receive additional protection because they can provide elevated access to systems and security configurations.

Where supported, Salama Estates may use multi-factor authentication, strong password requirements, account monitoring, device controls, and other authentication safeguards.

5. Security Alerts & Incident Response

Potential security incidents may be identified through automated alerts, system logs, access-control events, CCTV systems, reports from employees or residents, security personnel, or other monitoring mechanisms.

Examples of incidents include suspected unauthorized access, compromised accounts, malware, suspicious network activity, unauthorized entry, tampering with security equipment, theft, vandalism, or attempts to circumvent security controls.

When an incident is identified, Salama Estates may investigate the available evidence, contain the incident, protect affected systems or property, preserve relevant records, and take appropriate corrective action.

Where required, incidents may be escalated to management, security personnel, service providers, insurers, legal representatives, regulators, or law enforcement authorities.

6. Protection of Security Logs and Evidence

Security logs, access records, video footage, alerts, incident reports, and other security evidence are protected against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Where appropriate, security records may be preserved as part of an incident investigation. Access to such records is restricted to authorized personnel with a legitimate need to review them.

Information collected during an investigation is handled responsibly and only used for legitimate security, operational, legal, compliance, or investigative purposes.

7. Privacy & Responsible Security Monitoring

Salama Estates recognizes that effective security monitoring must be balanced with respect for privacy and applicable laws and regulations.

Monitoring activities are intended to protect people, property, systems, and information. Salama Estates seeks to limit collection and access to information to what is reasonably necessary for legitimate security and operational purposes.

Personal information and security records are handled according to applicable privacy requirements and internal policies. Security information is not made publicly available simply because it has been collected for monitoring purposes.

8. Security Review & Continuous Improvement

Salama Estates periodically reviews its cybersecurity and physical security controls to identify opportunities for improvement.

Security practices may be updated in response to emerging threats, changes in technology, security incidents, regulatory requirements, operational changes, and lessons learned from previous investigations.

The objective is to maintain a security environment that continuously improves the protection of Salama Estates communities, properties, people, information, and technology.
